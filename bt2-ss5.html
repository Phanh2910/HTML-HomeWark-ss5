TAYLOR SWIFT


Taylor Alison Swift (born December 13, 1989) is an American singer and songwriter . She is known for her narrative style, artistic versatility, and cultural influence . Swift is considered a prominent figure in popular music and a center of public attention .

Swift began pursuing a professional music career at the age of 14. She signed with Big Machine Records in 2005, becoming a country artist and has been referred to as the "princess of country music" ever since. At Big Machine Records, Swift released six studio albums, with the first four being sent to country radio, starting with her debut album Taylor Swift (2006). She followed that with Fearless (2008), which explored pop and country, and the singles " Love Story " and " You Belong with Me " which helped her gain mainstream recognition.
